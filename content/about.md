Hello, I’m Alp Yağcıoğlu, a computer engineering student at Politecnico di Torino.

This blog is my personal archive for writing about philosophy, literature, mathematics, pop culture, travel, and the places that leave a mark on me. I am especially interested in the unexpected connections between seemingly distant subjects—and in turning those connections into something concrete enough to revisit later.

The title, *From Whale Island to the Pequod*, reflects that journey between different fictional and intellectual worlds. There is no fixed route here; only subjects I find worth thinking about and stories I do not want to lose.

You can also find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/TheBoyWithDempseyRoll) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alp-yağcıoğlu-0335a9260/).
